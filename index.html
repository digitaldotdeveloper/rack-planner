Typical sizes: a 16 or 32-channel NVR is 1U, a 24-port switch is 1U, the two 16-port mini PoE switches are desktop units and share one 1U shelf. A tower server standing upright is about 44 cm tall, which is 10U, plus 1U for the shelf under it. Two towers fit side by side on one shelf (about 18 cm each across a 45 cm shelf). Measure your towers and change the U column if they differ. Cables: blue runs to computers and printers on the normal switches, and to phones or Grandstream access points on the PoE switches; orange is always a camera. The two 16-channel PoE NVRs power their cameras from their own rear ports, so only the cameras on the 32-channel NVR need the PoE switch. Keep 1U clear below the NVR stack. A normal line-interactive tower UPS does not mount on rails: stand it on the floor beside the cabinet and run its output to the PDU. Standing it inside the cabinet costs about 9U.
