Typical sizes: a 16 or 32-channel NVR is 1U, a 24-port switch is 1U, the two 16-port mini PoE switches are desktop units and share one 1U shelf. A tower server standing upright is about 44 cm tall, which is 10U, plus 1U for the shelf under it. Two towers fit side by side on one shelf (about 18 cm each across a 45 cm shelf). Measure your towers and change the U column if they differ. Keep 1U blank above and below the NVR stack.
